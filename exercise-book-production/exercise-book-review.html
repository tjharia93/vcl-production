Where exercise books stand

The products are standardised in ERPNext, and since today they have an opening finished stock. The production behind them is only partly modelled. A dev page records covers and inserts as stock moves. Ruling, folding and packing post nothing to ERPNext, and the floor route doesn't match the five steps the factory actually follows.

In good shape

  • Item names follow one standard (26 Oct). 392 chipboard and 18 Hi5 school items are done.
  • Every item has a Carton UOM, and the selling price is set per carton.
  • The BOM master is cut to 54 brand × size × ruling × pages rows, which cover 99.5% of units sold.

  • Covers (Material Receipt and Issue) and inserts (Repack) post stock from the dev page.

Not modelled yet

  • No job card is needed for the three stock brands (Prince, Swara, Hi5): the stock entries with a run number are enough. Job cards and specs (CPS) are only for school orders, in Phase 2/3 (Tanuj, 04 Nov).
  • The opening stock is at zero valuation, so the books carry no cost.
  • Packing is saved in the browser only. Nothing receives finished books into stock after the opening balance. Moves to stock in this push (Tanuj, 04 Nov).
  • The floor route reads Printing → Ruling → Sheeting → Collation. It has no Folding, Inserts or Packing. Fix: retag the route to the approved flow (Build plan, step 2b).

The dev pages are not in git. Several sessions edit them live over REST. On 03 Nov one session deployed from a stale copy and overwrote the Receive screen three times. Pull the live page before every change.

  • Only two links are stored today: the Repack (cover out and insert in on one entry) and order → delivery. Everything else is joined by reading item codes and names.
  • No batch number anywhere yet. Covers printed on one day can't be traced to the inserts or books they became. custom_eb_run is planned, not created.
  • The chain breaks twice: inserts → finished goods (packing isn't recorded) and finished goods → delivery (deliveries ship from Stores against a same-day receipt). Finished stock is a one-time count, not a running flow.
  • Where the numbers come from today: the dev page computes Order & stock in the browser, but it undercounts covers 2–3× and can't see Repacks. Compass Exercise Book Orders is built but not deployed. This tab uses the PC's one-off reads of ERPNext, which aren't refreshed. No report or saved query exists.
  • Recommendation: store the chain on the item, don't parse codes. Add fields (or a small "Exercise Book Set" record per brand | size | pages | ruling) for the cover, fold and insert items and the up-count, plus each book's insert item. Fill them once from today's codes (all 80 sets match). The dev page and Compass then read the fields. This also powers the "Item set per book" screen and a data-health check for missing twins.
  • Next links to add, in order: (1) the batch number on every floor entry; (2) the Packing entry into Finished Goods, carrying the batch number; (3) deliveries from Finished Goods, ending the same-day receipts; (4) paper → ruling in Phase 2/3.
  • One source for the numbers: a server-side report in vcl-production that reads stock balances, the ledger and the item links, so the dev page, Compass and this tab all show the same figures.
  • Who keeps it, going forward: the floor enters covers, folds, inserts and packing on the Exercise Book page, each with the batch number. Sales and dispatch keep orders and deliveries as now.

The production process, step by step

The five steps as Tanuj set them out. Each step shows what the factory does, what goes in and comes out, and how ERPNext and the dev page record it today. Anything marked "to confirm" has not been checked with the floor. No machine, owner or wastage figure is recorded for any step yet.

1

Ruling (inner papers)

Not in ERPNext
What happens
Plain paper is ruled on the ruling machines: single line, square line, graph, and so on.
In
Paper: NP newsprint and BP bank paper (the only two on the ruling log). Hi5 school uses Brilliant White Bank Paper (to confirm whether it is ruled on the same machines).
Out
Ruled sheets, A1 (the ruling log records A1 output only).
Where
Ruling stations R-01 to R-04 on the floor log. ERPNext has two workstations under Ruling: Ruling Machine 01 and Ruling 02. Neither has a single schedule line. 8 operators are on the log.
Recorded today
On a handwritten daily log, which is scanned to email. Every day at 10:00 an n8n job reads it with Claude Vision, appends it to a master CSV and posts it to #ruling_department on Slack. None of this reaches ERPNext.
Gap
Inner paper and ruling are not modelled at all. Ruled sheets are not a stock item, so the paper issued to ruling and the sheets ruled can't be reconciled. The ruling options on CPS (Single/Double/Square-Graph/Blank) don't match the real list.
2

Covers

Posts stock
What happens
Covers are printed several to a sheet. The standard is A5 = 3-up and A4 = 2-up.
In
Cover board: chipboard (CB), plus MNL and MGC covers (all non-chipboard covers are still to be standardised). Ink and plates.
Out
Printed cover sheets, item COV-<book code minus its first 4 characters>, counted as 1 Nos = 1 printed sheet. The up-count is part of the item name, and the page reads it with /(\d+)\s*up$/. A5 9-up and A4 4-up exist in the code but nothing uses them.
Where
Stage Sheet to Sheet Printing (step 30 on the exercise-book route).
Recorded today
The Cover Stock quick entry on the dev page posts a submitted Material Receipt into Stores - VCL. Remove posts a Material Issue ("used in binding").
Gap
Board and ink aren't consumed, so the receipt has no cost. Some older code reads "covers" as single covers, not sheets, and applying the up-count twice overstates capacity. No spoilage is recorded (the 04 Nov review proposes printed, usable and spoilage per run).
3

Folds (folded inner paper, an input)

Not recorded
What happens
Ruled sheets are cut and folded into the book's inner section. The page count sets how many sheets make one book (CPS requires pages in multiples of 4).
In
Ruled sheets from step 1.
Out
Folds: the folded inner section, counted in ups like covers and inserts (one fold set = 2 books A4, 3 books A5). Folds are an input to Inserts, the same way covers are. Item: FLD-<PNC|SWR>-<A4|A5>-<PPP>PG-<RULING>, the twin of COV and INS (80 created 04 Nov). Settled 04 Nov: counted in ups, like covers and inserts.
Where
To confirm: by machine or by hand. ERPNext has no Folding stage. The nearest stages are Sheeting (50) and Collation (60). The Collation workstations Collater 01 and 02 have no book work recorded.
Gap
There's no stage, no workstation and no count, so yield from sheet to book can't be measured.
4

Inserts

Posts stock
What happens
Covers and folds are inserted together to make the insert: covers + folds = inserts. To confirm on the floor: where stitching and trimming happen (a stitching machine is on the spares list).
In
Should be the cover (COV-…) plus the folds (FLD-…). Today only the cover is taken out.
Out
Insert item INS-… (Item Group Exercise Book Inserts). 1 cover sheet = 1 insert sheet. Book equivalents = sets × the up-count.
Recorded today
The dev page's Inserts tab posts a submitted Repack in Stores - VCL: the COV item goes down and the INS item goes up by the same quantity. Proposed: the same Repack also takes out the folds. One cover sheet goes with one fold set, since folds are counted in the same ups (A4 2-up, A5 3-up).
Gap
Collating, stitching, binding and trimming aren't modelled, and the inner paper is never consumed. It isn't decided when inserts are consumed into finished books.
5

Packing

Browser only
What happens
Books are counted into cartons. Pieces per carton depend on the page count.
In
Finished books and cartons.
Out
Packed cartons: 200pg = 66, 120pg = 102, 96pg = 120, 80pg = 144, 64pg = 162, 48pg = 204 per carton. 100-page packing is still blank.
Where
To confirm. ERPNext has no Packing stage for this line.
Recorded today
The dev page's Packing tab records full cartons, loose books and rejects, but only in the browser (localStorage). Nothing reaches ERPNext. Finished stock exists only as the 04 Nov opening balance.
In progress
A receiving backend on vcl-production branch feat/exercise-book-receiving (a84ef92, 03 Nov). It counts cartons plus loose books, takes books per carton from the item's Carton UOM, spot-weighs about 5% of cartons and flags any more than 5% off. It adds the child doctype VCL Stores Receipt Book Line. It is only on the PC (not pushed) and has no screen yet.
Gap
Nothing receives packed cartons into Finished Goods - VCL, so stock will drift from today's opening balance with the first delivery.

Scope and rules

The build for Phase 1: stock exercise books (Prince, Swara, Hi5), from printed covers and folds to packed cartons in Finished Goods, plus the checks on every selling document. Paper, ruling, school and customised orders come in Phase 2/3 (see Roadmap). Every rule here was agreed with Tanuj on 04 Nov 2026 unless it is marked open.

Rules the build keeps

  • Made to stock. Runs are planned by group (brand, size, pages, ruling), never one per Sales Order.
  • One place to enter each station. The duplicate tracker on /production-dev goes.
  • Every floor entry records stock in ERPNext. Nothing important lives only in a browser.
  • Covers are counted in printed sheets. A4 = 2 books per sheet, A5 = 3. One sheet makes one insert set.
  • Covers + folds = inserts. Folds are an input, named FLD-<PNC|SWR>-<A4|A5>-<PPP>PG-<RULING> (approved).
  • The run number travels on every entry (custom_eb_run on Stock Entry).
  • Checks run on the server (school or brand, unit and price), so every screen, import and API call is covered.
  • Code lives in git. Items, fields and settings are created in after_migrate, never in a patch. No live-only scripts.

Order to stock flow

Draft for discussion. Nothing here is decided or built. It's a starting point that maps each floor step to a record ERPNext already has, so the review has something concrete to change.

Made to stock, not to order. A work order can't be raised for every Sales Order. Production and sales run as two separate loops that meet at finished stock. Production fills stock by SKU, in runs. Sales draws stock down, order by order.

Production loop (by SKU, in runs)

Run plan per SKU (from total open demand − stock, or a minimum level)→ Ruling→ Covers (COV-)→ Folding→ Inserts (INS-)→ Packing receipt→ Finished Goods - VCL

Sales loop (by order)

Sales Order (Carton)→ Allot from stock (earliest delivery first)→ Delivery Note→ Sales Invoice

A dashed box is a record or step that isn't used for exercise books today. The only link between the loops is the shortfall per SKU: when open demand exceeds finished stock plus what's in the pipeline, that SKU gets a run. A run is planned by SKU, never by order.

The smallest version

  • Made to stock. Runs are planned by group (brand, size, pages, ruling), never one per Sales Order.
